How Trivia Royale 20 Works

  • 20-Player Lobby: Every match has 20 total combatants. Every player/bot starts with 3 lives (❤️❤️❤️).
  • 6-Second Matchup & Category Preview: Before every question, a 6-second VS screen reveals your opponent, the next question's category, equipped passive abilities, both players' remaining lives, and lets you arm Consumable Power-Ups.
  • 1v1 Pairings: Each round, surviving players face off with a 20-second timer (or 10 seconds if either player activates Sudden Death). If an odd number remain, one player receives a Bye.
  • Winning a Duel (Standard): Fastest correct answer wins. The loser loses 1 life (or 2 lives with Double Jeopardy or Buzzer Beater).
  • Survival Mode (🛡️): Available in Single Player and Private Online Rooms. Players and bots only lose a heart if they get the question wrong or run out of time—if both answer right, neither loses a life! Plus, answering correctly before your opponent awards +2 🪙 per question.
  • Passive Abilities (🔮): Permanent non-consumable perks bought in the Shop. Equip 1 at a time (or 2 with Dual Wielding). Bots also have a 7% independent chance each to spawn with Ironclad, Buzzer Beater, Last Stand, Lucky Draw, Buffer, Vampire, or Mulligan (max 1 bot per ability).
  • Bot Prestige Skins: Bots have a chance to spawn wearing random Prestige Skins based on difficulty: Very Hard (10%), Hard (5%), and Medium (2%).
  • Answer Streaks (🔥): Real players who answer 4+ questions in a row right and before their opponent earn a flame badge showing their live streak, which carries across games.
  • Ties & Double Misses: If both answer wrong (or time out), both lose 1 life. If both answer right in the exact same time, neither loses a life (and Lucky Draw grants +1 ❤️!).
  • XP, Levels & Skin Unlocks: Everyone starts at Level 0 (0 XP). Earn +1 XP per correct answer plus placement bonuses: Top 10 (+5 XP), Top 7 (+10 XP), Top 5 (+20 XP), Top 3 (+40 XP), 2nd (+60 XP), 1st (+80 XP), or Undefeated 1st (+150 XP). Leveling requires 250 XP (Lv 0→1), 500 XP per level (Lv 1–5), 750 XP per level (Lv 5–10), and 1,000 XP per level from Lv 10 onward.
  • Round 1 Exclusives & Bot Power-Ups: Juggernaut (1,000 🪙, start with 5 ❤️) and Speed Blitz (750 🪙, -0.5s on all answer times for the match) can only be activated on the Round 1 VS screen (5% chance a bot spawns with Juggernaut). Bots also have a 4% chance per duel to activate Double Jeopardy, Heart Bargain, Aegis Life Shield, or Sudden Death.
